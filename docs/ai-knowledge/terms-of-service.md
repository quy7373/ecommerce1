This is a nonbinding outline for the test project, not publishable legal terms. It does not identify a merchant, establish governing law, waive rights, allocate liability or form a customer agreement. Legal entity, jurisdiction, consumer obligations and enforcement wording are undecided.

## Proposed service description

The prototype supports browsing products, Google account sign-in, persisted carts, simulated orders, Stripe test-mode payments and general FAQ support. Product prices/availability can change before order creation; purchase snapshots are recorded when an order is created. A test payment is not a real commercial transaction. Commercial launch requires approved policies and operational readiness beyond this prototype.

## Accounts and access

Google supplies verified identity; the application does not ask for a store password. Customer and admin permissions differ and are enforced by the backend. Proposed acceptable-use rules would prohibit unauthorized access, payment manipulation, abusive requests and attempts to obtain other users' data. These are suggested terms topics, not approved enforcement provisions. Account suspension, deletion and appeal procedures need owner decisions and implementation work.

## Privacy and support automation

The proposed application records identity, cart and order details needed for operation. Card entry occurs in hosted Stripe Checkout. The FAQ assistant has no account/order/payment action tools and should receive no personal or payment information. No chat transcript is saved by default; external AI processing remains disabled until provider processing and retention terms are approved. This draft is not a privacy notice: controller identity, lawful basis, retention schedules, processors, international transfers and data-subject request procedures require separate review.

## Purchases, shipping and returns

Pricing/tax, payment acceptance, order acceptance, shipping coverage, delivery estimates, cancellation, returns and refunds must be specified in separately approved policies. The draft payment, shipping and return documents cannot be incorporated as final terms. Do not promise fixed rates, guarantees or rights from this outline.

## Intellectual property and legal review

The owner must determine content ownership/licenses, user content handling, permitted use, third-party terms, warranty statements, liability provisions, mandatory consumer rights, dispute process, governing law, changes to terms, effective date and contact details. Avoid adding a jurisdiction, arbitration clause, liability cap or automatic consent provision without owner/legal approval. A live checkout must not claim agreement to these unapproved terms.
